About this app

Everything you need for your next game night.

BLUFFᴮᴰ is a collection of fun, fast and chaotic party games made for playing together with friends.

Challenge your friends with games like Impostor, Mafia, Who Am I?, Truth or Dare, Would You Rather, Wrong Answer Only, Never Have I Ever, Charades, Perfect Circle, Chess and more.

Whether everyone is sharing one phone or you're playing different game modes, BLUFFᴮᴰ is designed to keep the group involved and the game moving.

More games and features are coming as the app grows.
